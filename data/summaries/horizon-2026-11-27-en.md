## [Nvidia halts AI server shipments to four Southeast Asian countries](https://t.me/zaihuapd/45392) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Nvidia has asked its major manufacturing partners to pause shipments of AI servers to Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand, and Indonesia, and assemblers such as Dell and Supermicro have told buyers in those countries that their orders cannot leave the factory. According to three people familiar with the matter cited by the Financial Times, the freeze is tied to a new round of US export controls, though it remains unclear whether it is a temporary measure. The four countries have become key transshipment points through which Chinese AI companies obtain Nvidia's most advanced processors, which cannot be exported directly to mainland China, so the pause directly threatens their access to frontier compute. It also shows how US controls are shifting from the chip itself to the finished server and cloud layer, putting hardware supply chains and OEM order books across Southeast Asia at risk. Nvidia typically sells GPU boards and modules that OEMs such as Dell and Supermicro integrate into complete AI servers, which is why the halt had to be communicated through assembling partners rather than to end customers directly. The new US Commerce Department rule reported last week requires regional cloud service providers to obtain export licenses within 90 days and to verify that their customers do not include restricted Chinese entities.

**Background**: US export controls have long barred Nvidia from selling its top-end AI accelerators directly to mainland China, prompting Chinese firms to route purchases through third countries. Chips usually reach those markets inside finished servers built by OEMs in Southeast Asia, which has made the region a focal point for US enforcement. In recent months Washington has widened its rules from hardware destined for China to remote access and cloud computing, requiring licenses for data centers worldwide that provide significant compute to Chinese-headquartered companies. The reported shipment pause is the first visible sign that those rules are now being applied at the factory gate.
