## [Anthropic Debuts on Nasdaq Under Ticker ANT, a Landmark AI IPO](https://www.reuters.com/business/finance/anthropics-historic-debut-put-ais-promise-peril-wall-st-test-2026-11-19/) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Anthropic priced its initial public offering at $83 per share, selling about 956 million Class A shares itself alongside roughly 23.65 million shares from existing stockholders, and began trading on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the ticker ANT on November 19, 2026, with the offering expected to close November 20. The stock opened at $111.50 but closed its first session at $97.17 — up 17.07% from the IPO price yet down 12.85% from the opening trade. ## [Anthropic says Claude solved two open problems in black hole physics](https://www.anthropic.com/research/claude-black-hole-physics) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Anthropic published a research post reporting that Claude (referred to in coverage as Fable 5.2) produced solutions to two open problems in theoretical black hole physics: a bootstrap calculation and an upper bound on black hole mass consistent with a given central charge. The claim was announced as a bullet point in a frontier model launch and accompanied by a blog post plus technical write-ups. If verified, this is a notable milestone for AI-for-science: a general-purpose language model contributing novel, non-trivial results in a highly technical branch of theoretical physics rather than merely summarizing existing literature. It fuels the broader debate over how much frontier LLM reasoning can accelerate or automate theoretical research, and over how such contributions should be credited and evaluated. Anthropic itself notes that neither result describes our own universe directly and neither has practical applications, though both feed the long-running effort to reconcile quantum mechanics with general relativity; the second result is viewed as the more significant because a theory predicting a black hole mass above that bound would fail to be a consistent theory of quantum gravity. Commenters also observed that the blog post and papers never use the word "prompt," leaving the human-vs-model division of labor unclear.

hackernews · cybermango · Nov 19, 17:30 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50563148)

**Background**: Quantum gravity is the sought-after framework that would unite general relativity (gravity, black holes, spacetime) with quantum mechanics, and it remains one of the biggest unsolved problems in physics. Much of the recent progress uses holographic duality (AdS/CFT), the idea that a universe with gravity can be described exactly by a quantum theory without gravity in one fewer dimension; "central charge" is a parameter of that dual quantum theory. A "bootstrap" calculation in this context is a notoriously laborious method that constrains a theory using only general consistency requirements such as symmetries and positivity, rather than by brute-force computation.

## [UK government admits it served Apple a secret encryption order](https://macanorak.com/uk-government-forced-to-admit-it-issued-apple-a-secret-encryption-order/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

A newly published Investigatory Powers Tribunal judgement confirms that the UK government issued Apple a secret Technical Capability Notice (TCN) demanding a way into its encrypted user data, ending nearly two years of official refusal to acknowledge the order. The tribunal also found that the media learned of the notice through confidential security briefings rather than a leak, while the contents of the notice itself remain classified. This is the first judicial confirmation that one of the world's largest device makers was targeted by a UK technical capability order, and it sets a precedent that such demands can be imposed and litigated in near-total secrecy. The outcome directly affects UK users of iCloud, and it signals to other governments that compelled-access orders against global encrypted services are viable policy tools. The notice was issued under section 253 of the Investigatory Powers Act 2016, which lets the Home Secretary compel a telecoms or postal operator to build or maintain interception capabilities; Apple refused to comply and instead disabled its end-to-end encrypted Advanced Data Protection feature for new UK iCloud users rather than weaken it. The judgement discloses the existence of the order but not its operational terms, and it is notable that the disclosure was triggered by reporting based on confidential briefings rather than by a whistleblower leak.

hackernews · Terretta · Nov 19, 16:21 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50562113)

**Background**: The Investigatory Powers Act 2016 is the UK's main surveillance statute, and a Technical Capability Notice is one of its strongest instruments: a legally binding order requiring a communications provider to ensure it can carry out interception or equipment interference. The Investigatory Powers Tribunal is the independent judicial body that hears complaints about unlawful surveillance by UK public authorities and has historically operated with heavy secrecy around national security matters. Apple's Advanced Data Protection extends end-to-end encryption to most iCloud data categories, meaning Apple itself holds no keys — which is precisely why a compelled-access demand put the company in conflict with UK authorities.

## [PostgreSQL 19 released with WAIT FOR LSN, delayed by AI-reported vulnerabilities](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Nov/19/postgresql-19/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

PostgreSQL 19 has been released about two months later than the usual September cycle, after the team worked through a surge of AI-reported vulnerability reports. The release adds the WAIT FOR LSN command, which lets a standby session wait until it has replayed the write-ahead log up to a specified log sequence number before running a query. WAIT FOR LSN gives applications read-your-writes consistency when reading from asynchronous standby replicas, without requiring synchronous replication and its latency cost. This is significant for read-heavy, geographically distributed systems that scale reads across replicas but still need users to see their own recent writes. A typical pattern is to capture the current WAL insert LSN on the primary after a write, store it, and then issue WAIT FOR LSN on the standby before querying; an optional TIMEOUT clause returns an error if the replica does not catch up in time. The approach adds extra round trips and requires application changes, so for some workloads reading from the primary may remain simpler and faster.

rss · Simon Willison · Nov 19, 20:43

**Background**: PostgreSQL uses write-ahead logging (WAL), where changes are recorded in a log before being applied to the database; each position in that log is identified by a log sequence number (LSN). Streaming replication ships WAL to standby servers asynchronously, so a standby can lag behind the primary and miss a write that a user just made. The read-your-writes problem is therefore common in replica-based architectures, and MediaWiki historically implemented a similar pattern for MySQL by saving the primary binlog position and waiting for replicas to catch up.

## [Emergency PHP Patch Follows Severe Vulnerability, Prompting Website Takedowns](https://t.me/zaihuapd/45233) ⭐️ 8.0/10

A severe PHP vulnerability that reportedly lay hidden for more than a decade prompted an emergency patch on October 21 and precautionary takedowns of government and commercial websites in the Netherlands, Denmark, Finland, France and elsewhere, with Wikipedia also temporarily shutting down some systems. PHP underpins a large share of the web—the post cites about 75% of sites by identifiable programming language—so a critical flaw can affect critical infrastructure, content management systems and ordinary businesses at once, and national cyber agencies in Europe, Australia, Japan and the UK urged immediate updates or temporary takedowns. The aggregation does not provide a CVE identifier, affected PHP versions, exploit conditions, or independent verification, and its editor’s note separately lists risks across PHP, Node.js, NGINX (including njs), Go, Rust and LiteLLM, with remote code execution cited among the concern types.

**Background**: PHP is a widely used server-side scripting language that powers many content management systems and web applications, so flaws in its runtime or libraries can have broad reach. Remote code execution (RCE) is a severe class of vulnerability in which an attacker can run arbitrary code on a remote machine over a network, often leading to data theft, malware deployment, or full system compromise. The post also references NGINX's njs JavaScript module and LiteLLM, an open-source LLM gateway, as part of a broader security-risk snapshot rather than as confirmed components of the PHP issue.

## [OpenAI models optimize chip design, boost AMD GPU PPA up to 14%](https://openai.com/index/advancing-chip-design-with-our-models/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

OpenAI announced that its models helped design its Jalapeño inference chip, reducing standard-cell area by about 13% for the vector unit and about 10% for the matrix unit, with the changes validated during chip bring-up. In collaboration with AMD, OpenAI reported up to 14% improvement in power, performance, and area (PPA) on flagship GPU IP versus a highly optimized baseline, and some design changes have entered manufacturing. OpenAI also announced a collaboration with Synopsys to train a GPT-Synopsys model. The announcement is a significant example of AI models being used in commercial chip design rather than only as research demos, potentially shortening design cycles and improving efficiency for the chips that power AI workloads. It also directly involves AMD and Synopsys, suggesting AI-for-EDA could become part of mainstream semiconductor workflows. The reported gains are block- or IP-level results relative to already highly optimized baselines, not full-chip improvements, and OpenAI did not detail the methodology or how much human engineering was involved. The Jalapeño figures refer to standard-cell area reductions in specific compute blocks, while some AMD design changes are validated and others have reached manufacturing.

telegram · zaihuapd · Nov 19, 09:30

**Background**: Jalapeño is OpenAI's custom inference chip developed with Broadcom, designed to improve performance and power efficiency for running large language models. PPA—power, performance, and area—is a core set of chip-design metrics: better PPA generally means a chip can do more work with less power and silicon area. EDA tools are the software used to design, simulate, and verify chips, and AI is increasingly being applied to tasks such as placement, routing, and optimization.
