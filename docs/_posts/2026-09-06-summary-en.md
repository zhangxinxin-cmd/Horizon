## [Isar Aerospace Reaches Orbit and Deploys Payloads on Second Flight](https://isaraerospace.com/press/history-for-european-spaceflight-isar-aerospace-reaches-orbit-and-deploys-payloads-on-second-flight) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Germany's Isar Aerospace successfully reached low Earth orbit and deployed payloads on the second launch of its Spectrum rocket, which lifted off from Andøya Spaceport in Norway. This marks the first orbital mission achieved by a privately developed rocket launched from Northern Europe. This milestone gives Europe and its customers a sovereign, privately operated launch option, reducing dependence on Arianespace and non-European providers. It also validates the emerging commercial launch startup ecosystem in Europe as a credible global competitor. Spectrum is a two-stage liquid-fueled rocket designed to carry up to 1,000 kg to low Earth orbit, and Isar Aerospace manufactures roughly 80% of the vehicle in-house near Munich. The second flight lifted off from Andøya Spaceport, located in the Norwegian Arctic.

hackernews · mpweiher · Sep 6, 07:21 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49584083)

**Background**: Isar Aerospace was founded in 2018 as a spin-off from the Technical University of Munich and is named after the river that flows through Munich. Historically, European orbital launches have been handled by institutional programs such as Arianespace/SpaceX competitors, so a privately developed rocket achieving orbit from a new spaceport signals a shift toward commercial launch capability on the continent. Andøya Spaceport thus becomes the second active orbital spaceport in Europe after Russia's Plesetsk.

## [Asahi Linux Announces Official Support for Apple M3 Chips](https://asahilinux.org/2026/09/m2-episode-1/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Asahi Linux has announced official support for Apple's M3-series chips, extending Linux compatibility to the latest Apple Silicon hardware. This announcement marks a major milestone for the open-source project. This is significant because it allows Linux users to run the OS on Apple's newest hardware, giving developers and enthusiasts more options. It also shows the project's steady progress even without official documentation from Apple. As with previous chips, the support relies on reverse engineering because Apple does not publish hardware specifications. Community feedback notes that practical limitations remain, such as lower performance in some compute workloads compared to macOS Metal and missing features like sleep and HDMI output.

hackernews · mdp2021 · Sep 6, 14:08 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49586698)

**Background**: Asahi Linux is a community-led project started by developer Hector Martin that ports the Linux kernel and related software to Apple Silicon Macs. Because Apple does not provide official documentation for its M-series system-on-chips, the project reverse-engineers the hardware. The project has gradually expanded from the first M1 models to newer generations, and this announcement extends its efforts to the M3 generation.

## [OpenAI Quietly Tweaked Astra Benchmarks Post-Release; Hallucination Rate Briefly Dipped](https://fortune.com/2026/09/04/openai-quietly-boosts-some-of-astras-evaluation-metrics-amid-rare-delay-in-publication-of-the-modeblog-post-announcement/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

On September 3, after a rare delay in publishing GPT-6 Astra's launch blog post, OpenAI repeatedly modified Astra's evaluation metrics. Astra's hallucination rate was temporarily lowered from 4.2 percent to 2 percent before being restored to 4.2 percent. This matters because silent changes to benchmark numbers after release undermine trust in AI model evaluations, especially when they come from a leading lab like OpenAI. Developers, researchers, and policy makers rely on these figures to compare models, so unannounced revisions affect industry-wide decision-making. The changes were not limited to Astra: GPT-5.6 Sol's ExploitBench score was raised from 5.5 percent to 11.5 percent, while Anthropic's Fable 5.1 math score was temporarily lowered by about 10 percentage points. OpenAI said the edits were meant to make the numbers represent its best estimate of model performance.

**Background**: Evaluation benchmarks like ExploitBench measure specific capabilities: ExploitBench assesses how far an AI agent can go in exploiting a real-world security vulnerability, rather than a simple pass/fail. Anthropic's Fable series is a line of frontier models, and scores on math and security benchmarks are widely used to compare competing systems. The incident highlights the growing debate over how benchmark numbers are reported and audited in the AI industry.

## [China Approves First AI-Assisted Innovative Drug](https://www.gelonghui.com/live/2653282) ⭐️ 8.0/10

China's National Medical Products Administration granted conditional market approval to Eisutefovir tablets (brand name: Aipusiwei), the country's first original drug developed with AI assistance, for treating mild to moderate COVID-19 in adults. The drug was developed in just three and a half years from target discovery to completion of clinical trials. This approval marks a major milestone for AI-assisted drug discovery, proving that AI can help bring a real-world drug to market in record time. It could accelerate innovation in the pharmaceutical industry and encourage wider adoption of AI in healthcare and drug R&D across China and beyond. The drug was jointly developed by Westlake University, Westlake Laboratory, and Westlake Pharmaceutical (Hangzhou) Co., Ltd. It received conditional approval, meaning further post-market studies may still be required, and it is specifically indicated for adults with mild to moderate COVID-19 infection.

telegram · zaihuapd · Sep 6, 09:10

**Background**: Traditional drug discovery is time-consuming and costly, often taking more than a decade from target identification to market approval. AI-assisted drug discovery uses machine learning and computational models to speed up target identification, molecular screening, and drug design. Although many AI-designed molecules have entered clinical trials, this is among the first approved original drugs in China developed with such an approach.
