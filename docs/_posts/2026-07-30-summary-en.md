## [Muon Mystery Solved: Old Results Reassessed](https://www.quantamagazine.org/physicists-solve-a-muon-mystery-now-old-results-dont-add-up-20260729/) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Physicists have resolved the long-standing muon g-2 anomaly, showing that previous experimental results need to be reinterpreted or corrected. This breakthrough, based on new theoretical calculations and the final data from the Fermilab Muon g-2 experiment, concludes that the measured muon magnetic moment is consistent with the Standard Model within 0.5 sigma. This resolution eliminates a major hint of new physics beyond the Standard Model, reshaping the search for particles and forces at high-energy colliders. It also validates modern lattice QCD methods, which are now crucial for interpreting precision tests of fundamental theories. The Fermilab Muon g-2 experiment finished data-taking in July 2023 and published its final results in June 2025, achieving a precision of 0.14 ppm. The previous discrepancy of about 4.2 sigma was reduced to 0.5 sigma after incorporating improved hadronic vacuum polarization calculations from lattice QCD.

**Background**: The muon anomalous magnetic moment (g-2) is a sensitive probe of the Standard Model, as quantum loops contribute to its value. For decades, experimental measurements at Brookhaven and later Fermilab showed a significant deviation from theory, sparking hope of new particles. Recent lattice QCD calculations have updated the theoretical prediction, largely eliminating the discrepancy.

## [AI Discovers Severe Weakness in Post-Quantum Candidate HAWK](https://startupfortune.com/claude-mythos-broke-hawk-and-the-nist-post-quantum-timeline-may-not-survive-it/) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Anthropic's Claude Mythos Preview AI model discovered a severe vulnerability in the NIST post-quantum digital signature candidate HAWK in about 60 hours, reducing its effective key strength from 2^64 to 2^38. This attack was previously missed by human experts for two years. This demonstrates AI's emerging role in cryptanalysis, potentially accelerating the discovery of weaknesses in cryptographic algorithms and impacting NIST's post-quantum standardization timeline. It also pressures organizations to adopt cryptographic agility and migrate to post-quantum security by federal deadlines. The attack on HAWK-256 is not polynomial-time, meaning larger keys remain secure, and HAWK has not been publicly withdrawn. The research also included an improved attack on a 7-round version of AES-128, but full 10-round AES-128 remains unaffected in production systems.

telegram · zaihuapd · Jul 30, 05:47

**Background**: Post-quantum cryptography aims to develop algorithms that remain secure against attacks from future quantum computers. HAWK is a lattice-based digital signature scheme selected as a candidate in NIST's additional digital signatures standardization process. Effective key strength, measured in bits, indicates the number of operations an attacker must perform to break the encryption; a reduction from 64 to 38 bits means the algorithm is far less secure.

## [MLVC: Cross-Platform Learned Video Codec Debuts](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1vb3xwd/mlvc_multiplatform_learned_video_codec_for/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Researchers from Microsoft introduce MLVC, the first neural video codec that achieves real-time performance (>100 FPS) on consumer NPUs from Apple, Intel, and Qualcomm while ensuring cross-platform compatibility by transmitting entropy model scale parameters through the hyperprior. This removes a major barrier to deploying learned video codecs in real-world applications, as previous neural codecs suffered from catastrophic decoding failures across different hardware platforms due to numerical inconsistencies. MLVC achieves over 70% MOS-based BD-rate improvement over hardware HEVC, and its open-source release on GitHub enables further research and development.

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/tanelai · Jul 30, 19:40

**Background**: Learned video codecs use neural networks to compress video, offering potential efficiency gains over traditional codecs like H.264 and AV1. However, they require consistent entropy model outputs between encoder and decoder; small numerical differences across platforms can break decoding. MLVC avoids this by explicitly transmitting scale parameters, eliminating the need for bit-exact neural network execution.
